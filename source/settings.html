Columns

Lists are dealt across the columns in turn. The window widens to fit.

Number of columns
Maximum tasks shown 0 draws everything, which a long list runs off the bottom of the screen with — a pinned window cannot scroll. Whatever is left out is counted at the bottom.

Order

Drag or use the arrows. This is the order lists are dealt into the columns. Unticked lists stay here, faded, keeping their place for when you tick them back.
